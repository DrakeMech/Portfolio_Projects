About

Hello! I'm Stefan, a 24 year old interaction designer and music enthusiast and instrument maker. I'm extremely passionate about creating interactive musical experiences and I love experimental work.

Of course, I also enjoy playing guitar and keyboard and also playing with digital synths. Sometimes I experiment with foley and other audio experiments such as sound design, but other times I focus on the tangible interface design conceptualize in my free time.

    Main Projects

    Oikotonika

    Oikotonika is a MIDI controller designed for expressive sonification through environmental interaction with the local environment.

    Tangible Feedback in DMI

    Material exploration of 7 prototype samples emphasizing the relationship between embodied gestural interactions and sonic expression.

    AR Reflective Plant Care

    An AR design project catered to build awareness of the user's historical interactions with their home plants.

    Non-Newtonian Fluid UI for Sonic Manipulation

    Thesis project exploring the inherent kinetic feedback of Oobleck, a non-Newtonian fluid, to enable novel forms of sonic manipulation through its non-linear properties.
