Gestural Meta-manipulation Exploration

Overview

This project uses as medium a camera and MediaPipe to explore gestural interactions and their manipulation. The theme of the interaction is centered on the concept of meta-manipulation. Meta-manipulation refers to the ability to manipulate multiple aspects of a artifact or group of artifacts, resulting in new forms of coping behaviour and adaptive responses to its environment.

In this project, I've looked further into Marleu-Ponty's phenomological view on refferencing our bodies to physical environments, within this context is a digital exploration of these concepts. Also, considering its intangible nature with the visual mediums, I tried to tackle our bodily relationship with digital visual artifacts in paralel with physical mediums.

Hierarchy Pushing

This video present a concrete example of how specific hand gestures are catered to hold, push, and manipulate both physical and digital objects in hierarchical context.

Individual Pushed

The Individual pushing interaction presents dimensionality and size of a particular object in refference to the user's hand. This creates a concrete relationship of scale, not as a hirerachy, by individual objects as reference. The downside of this approach was the orientation of the object relative to the user, which felt unnatural.

Nuanced Pushed

Now constituting a more nuanced act, an origin of direct manipulation, we can observe the readjustments of the grip in relation to the force applied to the artifact.

Push on an Angle

I also considered the impact of pushing objects at an angle, which introduced additional complexity in terms of control over the artifacts.

Final Concept

From this exploration, I noticed the value of this approach for VR/AR applications where hand gestures can be introduced more intuitevely through using physical probes. During this project time (1 week), I had this epithany of how we can refference metaphors prior to other daily physical interactions. Materials and physical boundaries are the natural cues refferencing to our visual apparatus, guiding us on formulating abstract manipulations in relation to digital mediums.
